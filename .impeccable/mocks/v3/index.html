01
Cohort-stratified prioritization of CRISPR–Cas9 sgRNAs for HDR-mediated correction of TP53 hotspot codons in cancer

S. Loke*, N. S. V. Movva, M. Hota

TP53 is mutated in roughly half of all human cancers. We prioritize guide RNAs for correcting recurrent hotspot codons, stratified across ovarian, pancreatic, and colorectal cohorts.

02
DriftScore — an anchor-relative metric for detecting quality drift in multi-turn multimodal generation

S. Loke*, N. Movva, R. Gudepu, N. Mburu

Iterative generation degrades output in ways standard no-reference metrics fail to track. DriftScore measures drift against the original anchor rather than between adjacent turns.
